Cookie Policy

This page describes the browser storage used by the Airevo demo storefront.

Demo notice: Airevo is currently presented as a demonstration storefront. The information below is sample policy content for demonstration only and must be reviewed and replaced with the business's final approved terms before accepting real customer orders.

Essential storage

The demo may store your basket and cookie-consent choice in your browser's local storage so those features continue to work.

Optional cookies

Analytics and advertising cookies are not intentionally enabled by this demo consent banner. If optional services are added later, this policy and the consent controls must be updated.

Changing your choice

You can clear the website's stored data in your browser settings. The consent banner will appear again after its saved choice is cleared.
