Privacy Policy

This sample policy explains how information may be handled when you browse the Airevo demo storefront.

Demo notice: Airevo is currently presented as a demonstration storefront. The information below is sample policy content for demonstration only and must be reviewed and replaced with the business's final approved terms before accepting real customer orders.

Information you provide

You may enter contact, account, delivery or enquiry details while exploring the demo. Do not submit sensitive personal or payment information in this demonstration environment.

How information is used

Information entered into demo forms may be used to demonstrate account, enquiry and order workflows. No real payment processing is enabled.

Storage and sharing

The demo may use browser storage and connected demonstration services to keep the basket, account and sample order experience working. This sample page does not promise a particular retention period or third-party processing arrangement.

Your choices

You can avoid submitting personal information and can clear site data through your browser settings. Contact the Airevo team for questions about this demonstration.
